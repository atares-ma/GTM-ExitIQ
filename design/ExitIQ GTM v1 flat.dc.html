Who is behind ExitIQ

A premier network of tech-focused M&A boutiques across Europe

Global Tech Mergers specialises in cross-border technology transactions, guiding founders and corporations from valuation to close. Each member is an independent boutique in its own market — the network is what carries a deal across borders.

ExitIQ is the network's readiness instrument. It applies the same criteria our partners apply when they prepare a technology company for sale, so a founder can see the picture before a buyer draws it for them.

Local partner, cross-border reach

Valuation through to close
